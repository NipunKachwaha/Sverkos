## How to Make a Roofing Website with AI in 5 Steps

Below, each step builds on the previous, moving from defining your roofing business to launching your website for local homeowners.

### 01. Define Your Services and Service Area

List out every service: roof replacement, repair, inspection, storm damage, gutters—each can become a dedicated page. Note the cities or neighborhoods you serve, because most people search for roofers by location.

Write down your differentiators—licenses, years in business, special materials, etc.—to give your site a unique feel.

This prep helps your site builder create relevant pages. Chatting with recent customers for their perspective can also help you find language that resonates with new homeowners.

---

### 02. Choose the Right Website Builder

Your builder choice affects how easily you can include service pages, galleries, and lead forms. Generic website creators usually force you to adapt a template from another industry. An AI builder like Sverkos auto-generates a site designed for roofers from the start, avoiding headaches and saving time.

Pick a platform that lets you describe your business in plain language, not one that makes you sift through random templates.

---

### 03. Add Pages Homeowners Want

Most homeowners care about a few main pages: services, about, contact, and a gallery showing your work. Specify you want a separate services page, a contact/quote form, and a photo gallery. The AI builder builds these out for you.

Launch with these essentials. Add things like a blog or financing page after the site’s live. An FAQ page can cut down on repetitive calls, and a financing section helps homeowners plan for big projects.

---

### 04. Build Trust with Photos, Reviews, & Local SEO

Homeowners spend big money and need to trust you. Prioritize real before-and-after photos—don’t rely on stock images.

Add customer reviews directly to the site and connect your Google Business Profile for local SEO. Include your city in titles and add a map showing your service area to boost local search ranking.

A short phone-filmed walkthrough video can earn more trust than perfect photos. Make sure your name, phone number, and address are identical everywhere online for credibility.

---

### 05. Launch and Track Leads

Test every form and phone link before launching so leads never fall through the cracks.

Connect your own domain to look professional—homeowners trust branded websites over generic subdomains.

Add basic analytics to see which pages turn visitors into calls or leads. After a month, check which pages are most popular and add detail or stronger calls to action as needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I build a roofing website without coding?**

Yes. With an AI builder like Sverkos, describe your business and get a ready-to-go site—no coding required. You specify what you want; the builder creates the pages for you. This unlocks web presence for smaller roofing companies.

**How long does it take to build?**

With an AI website builder, you can usually launch in a single session. Gathering great project photos and reviews is often the slowest step.

**What helps a roofing website rank in Google?**

Mention your locations naturally, connect your Google Business Profile, and keep your business details consistent online. A fast, mobile-friendly site also matters. Reviews and matching contact info further boost trust and search rank.

**Should I offer online quotes?**

A simple request form is best; most roofing jobs need inspections for accurate pricing. Collect contact and project info, follow up quickly, and if you have standardized pricing (like inspection fees), offer an easy online booking alongside your contact form.
