## Step 1: Define Your App

Start by clarifying your idea:
- Who will use your app?
- What specific problem does it solve?
- Is it for productivity, education, commerce, or something unique?

**Pre-prompt sanity check:**
- Is this a real problem for real people?
- Do current solutions fall short?
- Is there an existing audience or market?

**Popular categories for AI apps:**
- Business & productivity
- E-commerce & marketplaces
- Education
- Entertainment

Ask yourself:
- Who benefits most from this app?
- What’s the “aha moment” in the first minute?
- Any special user types or situations to consider?

## Step 2: Write Effective AI Prompts

More detail = better results. Be specific—tell the AI what you want!

**Bad prompt:**  
“Build me a social app.”

**Good prompt:**  
“Build a running tracker where users log runs, track distance and pace, share with friends, join groups, set personal goals, and monitor progress.”

**Prompt checklist:**
- Must-have features
- User roles and permissions
- What data is tracked
- App workflow (user journeys)
- External services/integrations needed

Look at existing prompt libraries for ideas.

**eCommerce example:**  
“Make a shoe store app: product catalog with images, filters for size/price, shopping cart, secure checkout, order tracking, and a clean mobile-first design.”

**Scheduling app example:**  
“Build an event scheduler with calendar views, RSVPs, notifications, and sync with Google/Outlook calendars.”

> Tip: The more specific the goals and key features, the better the result.

## Step 4: Preview Your App

Test drive your app as a user would.  
- Try out navigation and all main flows
- See how it works on mobile

Sverkos lets you preview for different devices. Take notes on what feels good and what needs work.

---

## Step 5: Test Your App

Thorough testing is critical!
- Try strange or invalid inputs
- Test for errors, speed, and data handling
- Double check user permissions and access

Focus on:
- Form validation
- Roles and access
- Third-party integrations
- Works well on all devices

Log every bug and improvement you find. Don’t launch until it’s solid!

#### Tweaking and Troubleshooting

Don’t rebuild from scratch! In Sverkos, ask the AI directly—for example, “Fix contact form submission” or “Filter isn’t working on data table.”

- Describe the bug or desired fix
- Share what you expect to happen
- Use discussion mode for back-and-forth refinement
- Restore from version history if needed

Review, tweak, and retest until the app works right.

## Step 6: Launch & Promote

Getting your app into users’ hands is a process:

**Checklist:**
- Make your app’s value obvious
- Optimize your store/landing page with great copy and keyword-rich descriptions
- Offer incentives (free trials, onboarding flows, launch notifications)
- Watch user feedback and data, and keep iterating

Launch isn’t a one-time event—plan ongoing outreach for growth.
